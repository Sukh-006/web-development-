About dreams

Dreams are involuntary successions of images, emotions, and sensations occurring in the mind, primarily during REM sleep. They are often vivid, story-like, and sometimes bizarre, functioning as a way for the brain to process emotions, memories, and daily experiences. Everyone dreams 3–6 times nightly, though most are forgotten.

Why do we dream?

While the exact purpose of dreaming is not fully understood, it is generally believed that dreaming helps the brain process emotions, consolidate memories, and solve problems by synthesizing information. Occurring mostly during REM sleep, dreams allow the mind to process complex feelings and re-examine experiences from the day.
